The system shall allow authorised users to acces a student's academic record
The system shall calculate the total number of credit's completed by a student based on recorded completed modules.
The system shall compare the student's completed credits against the credit requirements of their programme
The system shall determine whether a student has satisfied the required credit and programme completion requirements
The system shall identify outstanding credits or modules when a student has not completed all programme requirements
The system shall generate a completion letter for a student who satisfies all graduation requirements
The system shall make the completion letter available to the authorised student.
The system shall prevent a completion letter from being generated when the student has outstanding programme requirements 
The system shall authorised academic staff to update a student's academic results.
The system shall recalculate a student's completio status when their academic record is updated.
