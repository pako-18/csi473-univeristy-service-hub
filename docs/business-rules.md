#Business Rules

##Rule 1 — Credit Requirement
You'll be marked as eligible to complete your programme only once you've earned enough credits to meet (or go beyond) the total your programme requires.

##Rule 2 — Required Modules
You need to have finished every compulsory module in your programme — not just enough credits, but the specific modules that are marked as required.

##Rule 3 — Completion Letter
You can only request or receive your completion letter once you've met both of the above — the credit requirement and the required modules. If either one is missing, the letter won't be issued yet.

##Rule 4 — How Your Credit Total Is Worked Out
Your total credits are calculated by adding up the credit values of the modules you've actually completed and that count (i.e. valid, completed modules — not ones that are incomplete, invalid, or don't apply to your programme).

##Rule 5 — Seeing What You're Missing
If you haven't yet met all your programme's requirements, you'll be shown exactly what's still outstanding — whether that's extra credits you need to earn, specific required modules you haven't completed, or both.

##Rule 6 — Who Can Change Your Record
Only authorised academic staff (not students, and not just anyone with system access) are allowed to make changes to your academic record. This keeps your record accurate and protected.
