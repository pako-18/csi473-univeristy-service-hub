# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Introduction

This document identifies the stakeholders involved in the proposed University Service Hub project. It describes their roles, needs, concerns, and interactions with the proposed system.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Role | Needs | Concerns | Interaction with System |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Students | Primary users of the system | Accurate completion status; timely completion letter; clear information about outstanding requirements | Incorrect credit calculations; delays; privacy of academic information | Submit/identify their student information, view completion status, and receive the digital completion letter |
| Academic Staff | Academic personnel involved in programme requirements | Accurate information about student academic progress and requirements | Incorrect academic information; unauthorised changes | Review or provide academic information used in completion verification |
| Programme/Department Administrators | Manage programme requirements and student records | Reliable completion information; efficient verification process | Incorrect programme rules; incomplete records; processing errors | Maintain or verify programme requirements and review completion information |
| Examinations/Records Staff | Verify academic completion and official student records | Accurate credit calculations; reliable records; efficient processing | Incorrect results; unauthorised access; issuing a letter to an ineligible student | Verify completion, approve completion where required, and support issuance of the letter |
| University Management | Oversees university processes and service quality | Efficient process; accurate reporting; reduced administrative delays | System reliability; compliance; inaccurate reporting | Monitor system performance and completion-processing outcomes |
| IT/System Administrators | Maintain and support the system | Secure, reliable and maintainable system | Security breaches; downtime; data loss; system errors | Maintain the system, manage access, monitor availability and resolve technical problems |

## 3. Stakeholder Details

### 3.1 Students

  
**Role:**  
Students are the primary users who need to determine whether they have completed the requirements of their programme and obtain their completion documentation.

**Needs:**
- Accurate information about their completion status.
- Confirmation that their required credits and programme requirements have been met.
- Timely access to their completion letter.
- Clear information about any outstanding requirements. 

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect calculation of completed credits.
- Incorrect or outdated academic information.
- Delays in receiving the completion letter.
- Privacy and security of their academic information.


**Interaction with the system:**
- Provide or identify the required student information.
- View their completion status.
- View outstanding requirements where applicable.
- Receive the digital completion letter electronically.

### 3.2 Academic Staff

**Role:**  
Academic staff may provide or verify academic information relevant to determining whether programme requirements have been satisfied.

**Needs:**
- Accurate student academic information.
- Clear programme requirements.
- A reliable method of verifying academic information.

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect or incomplete academic records.
- Incorrect programme requirements being applied.
- Unauthorised changes to academic information.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Review relevant academic information.
- Verify academic requirements where required.
- Provide or confirm information used during completion verification.


### 3.3 Programme/Department Administrators

**Role:**  
Programme or department administrators are responsible for programme-related information and may support the verification of student completion.

**Needs:**
- Accurate programme requirements.
- Reliable student completion information.
- An efficient verification process.

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect programme rules being applied.
- Incomplete student information.
- Errors in completion decisions.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Maintain or verify programme requirements.
- Review student completion information.
- Support the completion verification process.

### 3.4 Examinations/Records Staff

**Role:**  
Examinations or records staff are responsible for maintaining or verifying official academic records and supporting completion processing.

**Needs:**
- Accurate academic records.
- Accurate credit calculations.
- Reliable completion verification.
- Efficient processing of completion documentation.

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect academic records.
- Incorrect credit calculations.
- Unauthorised access to student information.
- Completion letters being issued incorrectly.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Verify academic records.
- Review completion calculations.
- Confirm completion where required.
- Support the issuance of completion documentation.

### 3.5 University Management

**Role:**  
University management oversees the effectiveness and reliability of university services and administrative processes.

**Needs:**
- Efficient completion processes.
- Accurate information.
- Reliable service delivery.
- Appropriate oversight of the system.

**Concerns:**
- System reliability.
- Data security and privacy.
- Incorrect completion decisions.
- Inefficient administrative processes.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Monitor service performance.
- Review completion-processing information where appropriate.
- Use system information to support management decisions.

### 3.6 IT/System Administrators

**Role:**  
IT or system administrators maintain the technical operation, security and availability of the University Service Hub.

**Needs:**
- A maintainable system.
- Secure access controls.
- System monitoring capabilities.
- Reliable data management.

**Concerns:**
- Security breaches.
- System downtime.
- Data loss.
- Technical failures.
- Unauthorised access.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Manage system access.
- Monitor system operation.
- Maintain and update the system.
- Resolve technical problems.
- Support system security and availability.
