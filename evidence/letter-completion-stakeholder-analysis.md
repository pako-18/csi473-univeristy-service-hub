# Letter of Completion Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Objective

This document identifies stakeholders who interact with or are affected by the Letter of Completion verification process.

## 2. Stakeholders

The stakeholders investigated are:

1. Student
2. Department/Faculty Academic Officer
3. Registry/Examinations Officer
4. Employer or External Document Verifier
5. System Administrator

## 4. Detailed Stakeholder Analysis

### 4.1 Student

**Role:**
The student is the primary person whose academic completion status is being verified and who requires the Letter of Completion.

**Needs:**
- Accurate verification of programme completion.
- Accurate calculation/checking of required credits.
- Clear indication of whether programme requirements have been satisfied.
- Timely access to the Letter of Completion.
- Ability to identify outstanding requirements where applicable.

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect calculation of completed credits.
- Incorrect academic information.
- Delays in receiving the completion letter.
- Unauthorised access to personal academic information.
- Receiving a completion letter when requirements have not actually been satisfied.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Provides or identifies their student information.
- Requests or initiates completion verification.
- Views their completion status.
- Views outstanding requirements if applicable.
- Receives the Letter of Completion electronically when eligible.

**Information required:**
- Student identification number.
- Programme information.
- Completed modules/courses.
- Credits earned.
- Programme credit requirements.
- Completion status.

**Evidence/access available:**
- Academic record/transcript.
- Student information.
- Completion status displayed by the system.
- Digitally issued Letter of Completion.


### 4.2 Department/Faculty Academic Officer

**Role:**
The Department/Faculty Academic Officer supports the verification of academic and programme requirements for students.

**Needs:**
- Accurate programme requirements.
- Accurate student academic information.
- Reliable credit calculations.
- A clear method for identifying whether requirements have been satisfied.
- Efficient completion verification.

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect programme requirements.
- Incorrect student academic information.
- Errors in credit calculations.
- Unauthorised changes to academic records.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Reviews student academic information.
- Verifies programme requirements where necessary.
- Reviews the student's completion status.
- Supports or confirms completion verification.

**Information required:**
- Student identification.
- Programme requirements.
- Student academic record.
- Completed credits.
- Outstanding requirements.

**Evidence/access available:**
- Academic records available to the officer.
- Programme requirement information.
- Student completion information.
- Verification records generated by the system.


### 4.3 Registry/Examinations Officer

**Role:**
The Registry/Examinations Officer supports the verification of official academic records and the completion process.

**Needs:**
- Accurate academic records.
- Reliable credit calculations.
- Accurate completion status.
- Efficient verification of completion.
- Reliable completion documentation.

**Concerns:**
- Incorrect academic records.
- Incorrect credit calculations.
- Issuing a Letter of Completion to an ineligible student.
- Unauthorised access or changes to records.
- Loss or corruption of academic information.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Reviews academic records.
- Verifies completion information.
- Reviews or confirms eligibility for completion documentation.
- Supports the issuance or verification of the Letter of Completion.

**Information required:**
- Student identification.
- Academic record.
- Programme requirements.
- Credits completed.
- Outstanding requirements.
- Completion status.

**Evidence/access available:**
- Official academic records.
- Examination results.
- Programme requirements.
- Completion verification records.
- Issued completion documentation.


### 4.4 Employer/External Document Verifier

**Role:**
An employer or external document verifier may need to verify the authenticity of a student's Letter of Completion.

**Needs:**
- A reliable way to determine whether a Letter of Completion is genuine.
- Clear verification results.
- Access to only the information necessary for verification.
- A simple verification process.

**Concerns:**
- Forged or altered completion letters.
- Incorrect verification results.
- Exposure of unnecessary student information.
- Difficulty accessing the verification service.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Enters or scans the verification information provided on the Letter of Completion.
- Submits a verification request.
- Receives confirmation of whether the document is valid.

**Information required:**
- Letter verification/reference number.
- Verification code or QR code, if included in the proposed design.
- Limited information required to confirm authenticity.

**Evidence/access available:**
- Letter verification/reference number.
- Verification code or QR code, if included in the proposed design.
- Limited information required to confirm authenticity.


### 4.5 System Administrator

**Role:**
The System Administrator maintains the technical operation, security and availability of the proposed system.

**Needs:**
- Secure system access.
- System monitoring capabilities.
- Reliable data management.
- Ability to manage user accounts and permissions.
- Ability to maintain and troubleshoot the system.

**Concerns:**
- Unauthorised access.
- Data breaches.
- System downtime.
- Data loss.
- Incorrect system configuration.
- Technical failures.

**Interaction with the system:**
- Manages user access and permissions.
- Monitors system performance.
- Maintains the system.
- Performs technical troubleshooting.
- Supports system security and availability.

**Information required:**
- User accounts.
- Access permissions.
- System logs.
- System configuration information.
- Error information.

**Evidence/access available:**
- System logs.
- Access records.
- System monitoring information.
- Error reports.
- Administrative records.


## 5. Summary

The stakeholder analysis identifies the people and organisations that interact with or are affected by the proposed Letter of Completion verification process.
